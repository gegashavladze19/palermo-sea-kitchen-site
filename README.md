# Palermo Seaside Charm

Create a premium, modern, mobile-first restaurant website for Palermo Restaurant in Ureki, Georgia.

The website should feel warm, elegant, welcoming and suitable for a Georgian seaside restaurant. Do NOT make it look like a generic AI-generated restaurant template.

BUSINESS INFORMATION:

- Name: Palermo
- Type: Restaurant
- Location: 67 Ekvtime Takaishvili Street, Ureki, Guria, Georgia
- Opening hours: Every day, 09:00–00:00
- Cuisine: Georgian / local restaurant cuisine
- Known dishes include khinkali, khachapuri, salads, kebab, meat dishes, fish, kharcho, soups, chicken, pasta and potato dishes.
- The restaurant is associated with a relaxed atmosphere, generous portions and evening live music according to public listings.

IMPORTANT:
Do not invent phone numbers, prices, social media URLs, menu items, reviews, awards, claims, or other business information.
Where information is missing, create a clear placeholder that the restaurant owner can replace later.
Do not claim that information is official unless it is provided by the restaurant.
